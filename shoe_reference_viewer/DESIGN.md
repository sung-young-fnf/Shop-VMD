# Shoe reference study
Independent Three.js product viewer. Reuse cap viewer's restrained warm-white gallery and shared UI tokens. One cream LA low sneaker reconstructed in true curved geometry with open collar, separate tongue and laces, cream textile, suede overlays and gum sole. Product photos shown beside live object. Six presets: three-quarter, outer side, inner side, rear, top, outsole; material closeup seventh preset. Responsive controls and orbit/zoom/reset. All geometry inferred; photo color contains captured lighting. Do not claim measured PBR or manufactured accuracy.
